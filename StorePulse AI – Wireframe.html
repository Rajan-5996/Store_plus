23Store #412 is losing about ₹12,000 a day and is 150 orders short of break-even. Its demand and stock availability are in line with similar stores, but pick time doubles between 7 and 9 p.m. and the store pauses orders for around 85 minutes a day. Adding one picker in that window is the cheapest fix to test first.

What this means for #412 AI brief

15Competition is a contributing factor here, not the main cause. B opened a store 600 m away on 15 Sep with free delivery from ₹149 and a 9-minute promise, and #412’s orders dipped about 5% for two weeks before recovering. Our basket is 3.8% costlier, mostly in fruits & vegetables. The bigger issue is still the evening picking queue: it stretches our delivery to 12 minutes exactly when B is fastest, so fixing the queue also closes most of the speed gap.
